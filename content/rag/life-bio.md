# Roger Oria — Life & Bio

## Background

Roger Oria is a young Cuban software developer and entrepreneur. He grew up surrounded by real constraints — limited bandwidth, restricted payment gateways, geopolitical barriers — and turned those constraints into a deep intuition for building efficient, resilient software. Building in hard mode taught him more about product-market fit than any accelerator could.

## The Full Dev Cycle Philosophy

Roger defines himself as a "Full Dev Cycle" developer. This means he can take a product idea from zero to deployed: design the UI, build the frontend, write the backend, integrate AI, and ship to production. He does not specialize in one layer. He owns the whole thing. This is his core competitive advantage.

## Entrepreneurship

Roger is the founder of his own startup. He leads the development of social media and community products — Tones.platform.com, Empatando.com, and SocialClubs.com — that combine cutting-edge technology with genuine human value. He believes technology should empower people, not just entertain them.

## Personality & Work Style

Roger is self-taught in many areas and learns by building real things. He is a fast learner who picks up new technologies by shipping with them. He thinks analytically but also intuitively. He is a creative problem solver with a strong product mindset. He is deeply passionate about growth mechanics, viral loops, community building, and the psychology of engagement.

## Aspirations

Roger wants to become one of the most impactful tech builders from Latin America. His goal is to build products that reach millions of users, prove that geographic constraints breed innovation, and show that world-class software can come from anywhere.

## Current Focus (2025–2026)

Roger is simultaneously pursuing his Computer Engineering degree at Universidad Máximo Gómez Báez (2024–2027), building Tones, Empatando, and SocialClubs, expanding his AI and ML knowledge with PyTorch and self-hosted MLOps, and writing about software and product on his blog.

## Fun Facts

Roger has direct experience building software products under Cuban internet conditions, which means he optimizes for performance, low bandwidth, and resilience by default. He writes about product psychology, neurochemistry of engagement, and the future of freelance and community on the internet.
