# Roger Oria — Life & Bio

## Background

Roger Oria is a young Cuban AI Founder and engineer. He grew up surrounded by real constraints — limited bandwidth, restricted payment gateways, geopolitical barriers — and turned those constraints into a deep intuition for building efficient, resilient software. Building in hard mode taught him more about product-market fit than any accelerator could.

## The AI Founder Philosophy

Roger defines himself as an AI Founder: he takes AI products from idea to production solo — the model approach (fine-tuning, RL, or agent orchestration), the full-stack product, and the deployment. He owns the stack from the model up to the product. His mission is to become the #1 in AI, built in public through shipping: a personal laboratory producing ~30 AI builds a year.

## Entrepreneurship

Roger is the founder of Polygrow, his flagship — an AI-native business OS for solopreneurs. Around it he is rebuilding the substrate itself: Rogis (Redis for AI agents), Rustenwer (intelligence fabrication), Agentropy (agent observability), Domino RL (pure reinforcement learning research). He believes technology should make people more capable of being themselves, not just entertain them.

## Personality & Work Style

Roger is self-taught in many areas and learns by building real things — "learning by building": learn the minimum, ship immediately, study problems as they arise. He is direct and decisive, values honest evidence over hype, and prefers incremental MVPs over big up-front designs. He thinks analytically but also intuitively, and pairs product instinct with ML rigor (verifiable logs, fixed seeds, reproduction commands).

## Aspirations

Roger wants to become the #1 in AI — the most recognized name in AI, through building in public (open source, writing, talks) and making Polygrow a success, each feeding the other. He wants to prove that geographic constraints breed innovation and that world-class AI can come from anywhere — including Cuba.

## Current Focus (2025–2026)

Roger is simultaneously pursuing his Computer Engineering degree at Universidad Máximo Gómez Báez (2024–2027), building Polygrow and his AI laboratory (Rogis, Rustenwer, Domino RL, Agentropy, DeepBooks…), going deep on RL foundations toward agent post-training (RLHF, DPO, RLVR), and writing about AI and product on his blog.

## Fun Facts

Roger has direct experience building software products under Cuban internet conditions (~2-3 hours of connectivity a day), which means he optimizes for performance, low bandwidth, and resilience by default. His idea of fun: teaching an AI to play Cuban domino with zero human heuristics. He thinks he might be the Cuban John Wick dev.
